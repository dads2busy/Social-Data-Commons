A gravity-model index measuring how easily residents can reach employment opportunities, built by linking LEHD-LODES workplace job counts to Census block group centroids with hierarchical distance approximation and a 200-mile radius cutoff, then standardized to 2020 Census geographies via area-weighted crosswalks. Communities with low employment access face longer commutes, higher transportation costs, and fewer economic opportunities — insights that help workforce development agencies and transportation planners target investments where improved connectivity would have the greatest impact on economic mobility.
