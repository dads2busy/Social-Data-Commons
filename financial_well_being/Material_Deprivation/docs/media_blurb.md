The Townsend Material Deprivation Index combines four census-derived indicators — unemployment, overcrowding, lack of vehicle access, and renter-occupied housing — into a z-score composite rescaled to 0–1, standardized to 2020 Census geographies using area-weighted boundary crosswalks for consistent multi-year comparison. This index helps public health and social services agencies identify neighborhoods where compounding disadvantages in employment, housing, and transportation create the greatest barriers to well-being.
