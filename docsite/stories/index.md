# Data stories

Data stories apply the commons to real local issues. Each one starts from a
question raised by stakeholders in Arlington or Fairfax County, and shows how
several measures, read together, tell a story that no single measure can.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-wifi: **Equity of access to broadband**

    ---

    Internet speeds are high everywhere in Arlington and Fairfax. Adoption is
    not. Combining speed, adoption, and price-to-income data shows the gap is
    about affordability, not infrastructure.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](broadband.md)

-   :material-hospital-box-outline: **Health equity**

    ---

    Is access to urgent care equitable in the National Capital Region? From a
    count of facilities, to drive times, to a population-aware catchment
    score, filtered by income and demographics.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](health-equity.md)

-   :material-store-outline: **Business diversity**

    ---

    Patterns in minority business ownership in Fairfax County, by industry
    and over time, and whether minority-owned businesses employ more minority
    workers.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](business-diversity.md)

-   :material-food-apple-outline: **Food security**

    ---

    A sub-county measure of food insecurity, built from a local cost of living
    and a synthetic table of households by income and size in every census
    tract.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](food-security.md)

</div>

The exhibits in these stories were produced for the original Social Impact
Data Commons dashboard. The same measures are available on the current
[National Capital Region dashboard](https://dads2busy.github.io/national_capital_region_data/).
