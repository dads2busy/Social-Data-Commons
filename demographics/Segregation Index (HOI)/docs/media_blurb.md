A measure of residential racial and ethnic segregation computed as the Entropy Index (Theil's H) across eight race/ethnicity categories, standardized to 2020 Census geographies using area-weighted boundary crosswalks so that trends can be compared across years without distortion from redistricting. Segregation remains one of the strongest predictors of disparities in health, education, and economic mobility, making this dataset essential for policymakers targeting place-based interventions to reduce structural inequity.
