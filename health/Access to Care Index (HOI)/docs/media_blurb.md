A composite index measuring access to primary care by linking CMS Medicare physician data to census tracts via HUD ZIP-to-tract crosswalks, computing population-to-physician ratios within 30 OSRM-derived driving miles, and combining with ACS uninsured rates into a z-score composite on 2020 Census geographies. This multi-source integration — travel time matrices, provider enrollment files, and insurance estimates — produces a unified accessibility measure that no single federal dataset provides. Identifying communities with both provider shortages and high uninsured rates enables targeted policy interventions that can reduce preventable hospitalizations and improve population health.
