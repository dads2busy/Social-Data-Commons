Spatial accessibility to primary care physicians at the block group level computed using three floating catchment area variants (2SFCA, E2SFCA, and 3SFCA with Gaussian decay) that account for both provider supply and population demand within OSRM-derived travel time catchments on 2020 Census geographies. The three-step FCA with quadratic weight normalization represents a methodological upgrade over simpler provider-to-population ratios by capturing the competitive dynamics of shared catchments. Primary care is the front line of preventive health, and identifying communities with poor spatial access helps health workforce planners and state health departments target provider recruitment where it is needed most.
