Spatial accessibility to OB-GYN physicians at the block group level computed using three floating catchment area variants (2SFCA, E2SFCA, and 3SFCA with Gaussian decay), linking CMS provider enrollment data to female population age 15+ as the demand measure within OSRM travel time catchments on 2020 Census geographies. The gender-specific demand weighting and travel-time-based catchments capture accessibility barriers that designation-based metrics like HPSAs do not reflect at sub-county resolution. Communities with limited OB-GYN access face higher rates of delayed prenatal care and adverse birth outcomes, making this dataset critical for maternal health policy and reproductive health equity initiatives.
