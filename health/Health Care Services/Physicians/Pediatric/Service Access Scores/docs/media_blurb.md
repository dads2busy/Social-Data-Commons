Spatial accessibility to pediatric physicians at the block group level computed using three floating catchment area variants (2SFCA, E2SFCA, and 3SFCA with Gaussian decay), linking CMS provider enrollment data to age-specific child population demand (ages 0–17) within OSRM travel time catchments on 2020 Census geographies. The demand-weighted catchment approach reveals accessibility gaps that simple provider counts or county-level ratios miss, particularly in suburban and rural areas where providers cluster in distant population centers. Children in communities with poor access to pediatricians face delayed preventive care, missed vaccinations, and greater reliance on emergency departments, making this dataset valuable for health workforce planning and Medicaid network adequacy assessments.
