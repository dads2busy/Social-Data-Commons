County-level prenatal care adequacy using the Kotelchuck Index, computed from two complementary sources: exact classification from NCHS natality microdata for 2014–2020, extended through 2024 by projecting county-specific trends from CDC WONDER trimester-of-entry queries. This hybrid approach maintains methodological continuity across data source transitions and provides more timely estimates than waiting for microdata releases. Adequate prenatal care is strongly associated with reduced preterm birth and infant mortality, making this dataset critical for maternal health policy, Medicaid program evaluation, and targeting outreach to underserved communities.
