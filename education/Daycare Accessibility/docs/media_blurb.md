Block-group-level childcare accessibility measures — including drive time to the nearest provider, total licensed capacity, and three-step floating catchment area ratios of available seats per child stratified by age group — computed using OSRM-based travel time matrices and VDSS facility data on 2020 Census geographies. The age-stratified floating catchment area approach accounts for both supply and demand within realistic driving catchments, revealing gaps that simple provider counts miss. Childcare access is a critical determinant of labor force participation, particularly for women and low-income families, making this dataset valuable for early childhood policy and equity-focused investment planning.
