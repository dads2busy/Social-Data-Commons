The Housing + Transportation Affordability Index independently reproduced from first principles using CNT's published regression methodology applied to ACS demographics, LEHD employment data, GTFS transit schedules, and TIGER geographies at block group resolution on 2020 Census boundaries. This independent reproduction enables annual updates (2015–2024) and exposes intermediate measures — housing cost share, transportation cost share, auto ownership, and transit use — that the original CNT dataset does not publish at this granularity. Traditional affordability measures that consider only housing costs miss the transportation burden that forces many families in car-dependent areas above the 45% affordability threshold, making this dataset essential for land use planning and affordable housing siting decisions.
