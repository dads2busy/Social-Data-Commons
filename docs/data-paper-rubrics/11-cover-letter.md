# Cover Letter: Rubric and Instructions

## Purpose
Outlines the research context and why the manuscript is appropriate for Data & Policy. Submitted via web form (plain text), not as a file upload.

## Requirements
- 3-4 paragraphs
- Plain text (no LaTeX formatting)
- Addressed to "Dear Editors,"

## Structural Template

### Paragraph 1: Submission statement (1-2 sentences)
"We submit the enclosed manuscript, '[Title],' for consideration as a Data Paper in Data & Policy."

### Paragraph 2: The policy data gap (3-4 sentences)
What information do policymakers lack? What are the consequences of this gap? Frame as a governance problem, not a research problem.

### Paragraph 3: What the dataset provides (3-4 sentences)
What the dataset measures, at what resolution, for what geography and time period. Mention if it is already in use (dashboard, agency). Include GitHub URL.

### Paragraph 4: Fit with journal (3-4 sentences)
Explain why this manuscript belongs in Data & Policy specifically. Connect to the journal's mission ("promote the use of data in an ethical, responsible, and efficient manner for policy"). Name specific policy actions the dataset enables.

### Closing (2 sentences)
"The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere and all authors have approved the submission. The data are deposited on Zenodo and freely available for download."

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Names the journal and article type
- [ ] Frames the gap as a policy problem
- [ ] Connects to D&P's mission
- [ ] Includes GitHub and Zenodo URLs
- [ ] States no competing submissions
- [ ] Professional, concise tone
